THE PILAS APPROACH

A little clarity, before you clean.

Content Credentials describe a file’s origin and editing history. Pilas reads the embedded C2PA structures and creates a separate copy with supported credentials removed.

  1. Add your files. Choose files or drop them into the workspace.
  2. Inspect the details. Select a file to see its detected credentials and cleanup method.
  3. Clean and download. The output is scanned again before it’s made available.
Removing embedded credentials does not erase external records, invisible watermarks, or ordinary EXIF metadata. Metadata shown here is parsed; cryptographic signatures and trust are not validated.

Built from the C2PA 2.4 specification .
